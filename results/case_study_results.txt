BET VOLATILITY PREDICTION - CASE STUDY RESULTS
==============================================
Date: November 18, 2025
Model: XGBoost Baseline (100 trees, depth=5, lr=0.1)

DATASET
-------
Total observations: 3,533 (after feature engineering)
- Training: 2,236 days (2010-2019)
- Validation: 702 days (2020-2022)
- Test: 497 days (2023-2024)

Features: 12 technical indicators
- 5 lagged volatilities
- 4 moving averages
- RSI, momentum, volatility of volatility

Target: Binary classification (75th percentile threshold)
- Positive class (high vol): ~25%

INTERPRETATION
--------------
✅ Strengths:
- Model successfully learns patterns (training AUC 0.98)
- Test AUC 0.68 is reasonable baseline without Google Trends
- VoV (volatility of volatility) emerges as top predictor
- Good precision on low volatility detection (0.80)

⚠️ Limitations:
- Overfitting observed (training 0.98 vs test 0.68)
- Poor recall on high volatility spikes (only 12% detected)
- Class imbalance challenges (25% positive class)
- No behavioral features (Google Trends) yet included

approach, achieving test AUC 0.68 with only technical indicators.

value of Google Trends behavioral features in the full study.
